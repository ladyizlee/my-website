Who Are We, Really?

Okay, so you probably think we're a reaction channel. 📦 We get it! There are cameras, there are BTS videos, there are faces. But that's not really how we identify ourselves... Let us explain:

We're Liz & Kelly: Back in August 2025, we were merely two STAYTINY who decided to press record while we fell for a whole new fandom. What started as "let's just record, maybe people will relate to us" turned into our ongoing BTS deep dive series which is quickly approaching 100 episodes, and we're documenting every step of becoming ARMY in real time, confusion, crying, and chaos included.

We aren't experts. In fact, we both have full time day-jobs, unrelated to this! All of our content remains free to access, which is never changing. We're not here to tell you how to feel about a song. We're here to learn out loud, ask the silly questions, interpret the art and discuss it openly together, and see what happens when two PLePs (Platonic Life Partners / besties/ etc.) fall down the BTS rabbit hole together. This community is everything to us, and we hope it becomes a safe space for many more to feel comfy landing on.

The best part?

We never expected this many people to join and follow our journey. But along the way we found you: the comments, the watchparties, the livestream bingo, the stims, THE EDITS, the Discord & Patreon friends who feel like family now, from all around the world. This isn't a channel with an audience. It's a community that we're lucky to be growing with.

We have big plans and even bigger dreams for our brand--inspired by one of our favorite writers, Yoongi, and motivated by another, RM. So don't lose us! Follow everywhere so you can join us on this ride.

Join our chaos. Embrace being "too much." 💜
-Liz & Kelly
